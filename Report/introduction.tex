With the rapid development of the Internet, e-mail has become a fast and convenient 
methods of communication and has been widely used in our daily lives. 

However, the popularity of e-mail has also brought about a problem that cannot be
ignored: spam. Unscrupulous businessmen and criminals have seized this communication 
medium of e-mail and started to send spam with commercial advertisements, and malicious
or illegal files to users on a large scale\cite{kumar_email_2020}. Spam not only occupies network resources 
and causes network congestion, but also affects people's normal work and life.

Therefore, how to quickly and accurately check whether an email is spam or not 
becomes a major topic worthy of research. It can protect people's property and 
privacy security  and also improve people's work and life efficiency. 

In this paper, we will try to solve spam text detection, using the latest TREC 2007 spam email
dataset\cite{trecspam2007} to train our model. We will use the
BERT model as the baseline model and compare
it with the GPT model to find out which model is
more suitable for spam email detection.
