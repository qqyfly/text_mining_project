\subsection{Transformer \& Attention}

The transformer architecture\cite{vaswani_attention_2023} revolutionized natural language processing by 
replacing traditional recurrent and convolutional layers with a mechanism based 
entirely on self-attention. This innovation enables the model to process entire 
text sequences in parallel, capturing long-range dependencies and contextual 
relationships more effectively than previous architectures. Figure 1 shows the 
architecture of the transformer model, it consists two parts, encoder and decoder.

Building upon the foundational Transformer architecture, BERT (Bidirectional 
Encoder Representations from Transformers) and GPT (Generative
Pre-trained Transformer) have emerged as two of
the most influential models in the field of natural
language processing (NLP).

BERT\cite{koroteev_bert_2021} is designed as a bidirectional deep learning model that captures contextual 
information from both the left and right sides of a given word in a sentence.
This bi-directionality enables BERT to gain a deeper understanding of language structure,
making it particularly suitable for tasks such as question answering, 
named entity recognition, and sentence classification. 
BERT is trained using the Masked Language Model (MLM) objective \cite{koroteev_bert_2021},where a subset of 
words in a sentence are randomly masked and predicted based on the surrounding context,
and the Next Sentence Prediction (NSP) task, which helps the model learn relationships between sentences.

In contrast, GPT and its subsequent iterations (GPT-2, GPT-3, and GPT-4) adhere to
an autoregressive modelling approach.\cite{zhao_survey_2025}. GPT generates text 
sequentially by predicting the next token based on the previous tokens in a left-to-right manner. 
Unlike BERT, which uses bidirectional attention, GPT uses unidirectional attention, 
limiting its focus to the previously generated tokens in the sequence.
The model is pre-trained using the Causal Language Modeling (CLM) objective\cite{zhao_survey_2025}, where it learns to predict 
the next word given a context, making it very effective in text generation, 
dialogue systems, and creative content generation.

Although both BERT and GPT leverage the self-attention mechanism inherent in Transformers, 
they employ different architectural components and training methods, resulting in 
unique capabilities and applications.
