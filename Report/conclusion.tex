In this study, we aimed to compare the performance of BERT and GPT-2 in the task of spam email classification. 
Given their distinct architectures—BERT being a bidirectional transformer and GPT-2 being an autoregressive 
model—we hypothesized that their ability to classify spam emails would differ significantly.

\subsection{Key Findings}

After training and evaluating both models, we found that BERT significantly outperformed GPT-2 
in classifying spam emails. The primary reasons for this performance gap stem from the 
fundamental differences in how these models process text:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Bidirectional vs. Unidirectional Processing: BERT's ability to consider both past and future tokens in a text sequence allowed it to develop a richer contextual understanding. In contrast, GPT-2's left-to-right processing limited its ability to capture dependencies that extend beyond previous words, leading to a weaker grasp of the full email content.
    \item Optimization for Classification: BERT’s pretraining objectives, including masked language modeling and next-sentence prediction, made it inherently better suited for classification tasks. Its [CLS] token serves as an effective representation for the entire input text, simplifying fine-tuning for spam classification. GPT-2, primarily designed for text generation, lacks a specialized mechanism for holistic text representation, making it less effective for classification.
    \item Token Representation and Attention Mechanisms: BERT processes entire email texts simultaneously, enabling it to establish long-range dependencies across the input. GPT-2, by contrast, processes tokens sequentially, which may cause it to overlook crucial spam indicators located later in the text.
    
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Extent to Which the Problem Was Solved}

Beyond just the comparative performance of BERT and GPT-2, we gained several insights from this study:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Model Selection Matters: Choosing the right transformer model based on the specific NLP task is crucial. While GPT-2 excels at text generation, BERT is more effective for classification.
    \item Pretraining Objectives Influence Performance: The way a model is pretrained significantly impacts its downstream performance. Classification tasks benefit from bidirectional training objectives.
    \item Interpretability and Efficiency: BERT’s structured output makes it easier to interpret classification decisions compared to GPT-2’s token-by-token processing, which lacks a global representation of the input.
\end{itemize}
